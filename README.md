AgriSetu is a digital platform designed to simplify the agricultural procurement process for farmers by reducing long waiting times and providing transparent, real-time information about procurement schedules and queues.

The platform enables farmers to register, select nearby procurement centres, book convenient time slots, and track their queue status in real time. It also provides notifications and procurement/payment tracking to keep farmers informed throughout the process.

AgriSetu aims to make agricultural procurement more efficient, transparent, and farmer-friendly by combining digital registration, slot booking, real-time queue management, notifications, and procurement tracking in a single platform.

Key Features:
• Farmer registration and profile management
• Procurement centre selection
• Online slot booking
• Real-time queue status
• SMS/App notifications
• Procurement status tracking
• Payment status tracking
• Multilingual and farmer-friendly interface
• Centralized dashboard for procurement management

Problem Statement:
Farmers often face long waiting times and lack timely information about procurement schedules and queue status at procurement centres. AgriSetu addresses this problem through a technology-driven solution that improves coordination between farmers and procurement centres.
